BABY COMPANION

User Guide

Your everyday companion for food diversification, health tracking, and your baby’s growth.

  • Simple every day — Record a meal, measurement, or note in just a few steps.
  • Designed for multiple babies — Each profile keeps its own meals, measurements, consultations, and reports.
  • Useful reports — Prepare a Diversification Report summary before a consultation.
  • Context-aware Assistant — Find information or prepare an action, always with your confirmation.

Version 1.0 - Parent Guide

STEP 0

Quick start

The 5 essential steps to get started with Baby Companion.

  1. Create your account with your first name, email address, and a password.
  2. Confirm your email address using the code you receive.
  3. Create your first baby’s profile.
  4. Record meals, foods, consultations, and measurements over time.
  5. Before a consultation, prepare a Diversification Report from the Reports area.

Key point

Baby Companion is designed to stay simple: detailed information is requested only when it is useful. You always stay in control of what is recorded or shared.

STEP 1

Create your account

A short sign-up process followed by email address confirmation.

  1. Enter your first name, email address, and a password of at least 8 characters.
  2. Tap Continue.
  3. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code sent to your email.
  4. Once your email is confirmed, continue directly to creating your first baby profile.

Didn’t receive the email?

You can request a new code after the indicated delay, or correct the email address if it contains an error.

Welcome email

After confirmation, Baby Companion can send you a welcome email with a link to this guide and its PDF version.

Forgot password

  1. From the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password.
  2. Enter your email address.
  3. Open the secure link received by email.
  4. Choose a new password and confirm it.

STEP 2

Create and manage baby profiles

Each baby has their own history and their own data.

  1. After confirming the account, enter the first baby’s name, date of birth, and sex.
  2. You can add a photo, but it is optional.
  3. To add another baby later, open My baby and use the add-profile option.
  4. Switch babies using the selector at the top of the app.

Data isolation

A baby’s meals, foods, measurements, consultations, vaccinations, reports, and notes are never mixed with another profile’s data.

Unique first name within the account

Two baby profiles in the same account cannot have exactly the same first name. This reduces the risk of confusion.

Edit a profile

  • Open My baby, then select the relevant profile.
  • Update the information you want, then save.
  • If you leave a form with unsaved changes, Baby Companion asks for confirmation.

STEP 3

Understand the Home screen

Home summarizes the day and gives you quick access to the most useful actions.

  • Today — Meals and events recorded for the current day.
  • Next consultation — Upcoming appointment date with quick access to its details.
  • Daily note — A simple space for keeping a free-form observation.
  • Quick actions — Add a meal, a food introduction, a note, or a measurement.

Yesterday, today, tomorrow

The dashboard lets you review the recent past and upcoming days. Meals that were actually recorded remain separate from meals that are only planned.

Main navigation

  • Home: a summary of the day.
  • Food: meals, planning, foods, and shopping list.
  • Health: consultations, measurements, vaccinations, and growth.
  • My baby: profile, reports, and information related to the active baby.

STEP 4

Food: meals, planning, and catalog

Track what was actually eaten while planning upcoming meals.

Record a meal

  1. Open Food, then Meals, or use Add from Home.
  2. Choose the meal type and time.
  3. Select one or more foods from the catalog.
  4. Save the meal, then if needed indicate how it went: Very well, Well, A little, or Refused.

Today’s meals only

The Meals screen focuses on the current day. To prepare a future meal, use Planning.

Plan a meal

  1. Open Planning.
  2. Choose a future date and time.
  3. Add the planned foods.
  4. The meal remains Planned until it is actually recorded on the relevant day.

Food catalog

  • Instant search by name.
  • Filters by category and status.
  • Main statuses: To discover, Introduced, Observed reaction.
  • You can add a custom food if it is not available in the catalog.

STEP 5

Observed reactions and shopping list

Keep factual observations and prepare your shopping.

Observed reaction

  1. Open the relevant food details.
  2. Choose Add an observed reaction.
  3. Describe only what you actually observed.
  4. Then review the observation history from the food details.

Important

Baby Companion does not provide a diagnosis and does not interpret a reaction as normal or abnormal. If you have a medical concern, contact a healthcare professional.

Shopping list

  • Generate a list from planned meals.
  • You can also add items manually.
  • Edit quantities and units: piece, g, kg, jar, pack, bottle, box, or sachet.
  • Check off items you have already bought.
  • Share the list using your phone’s sharing options.

STEP 6

Health: consultations, measurements, and vaccinations

A practical record for keeping your baby’s health history.

Consultations

  • Record past consultations and the next upcoming consultation.
  • Add a reason or note if needed.
  • Measurements taken during a consultation remain linked to that consultation.
  • From the upcoming consultation, prepare a Diversification Report when useful.

Home measurements

  1. Open Health, then Measurements.
  2. Choose Add a measurement.
  3. Enter at least one value: weight, height, or head circumference.
  4. Save the date and the available values.

A measurement can be partial

You do not have to enter weight, height, and head circumference at the same time. At least one value is enough.

Vaccinations

  • Add a vaccination from a consultation or manually.
  • Record the vaccine, dose, date, and an optional note.
  • Baby Companion keeps the history but does not generate a personalized vaccination schedule in V1.

STEP 7

Growth and reference curves

Visualize progress without turning the curves into a diagnosis.

  • Choose the metric: weight, height, or head circumference.
  • Change the displayed period as needed.
  • Tap a representative point to display its date and value.
  • Use the reference view to compare the baby’s measurement descriptively with a visual reference.

Careful interpretation

The curves help you follow changes over time. Baby Companion does not classify growth as normal or abnormal and does not replace a pediatrician’s interpretation.

STEP 8

Diversification Reports

Prepare a clear summary before a consultation.

  1. Open Reports from My baby.
  2. Choose the start and end dates.
  3. Select Summary or Detailed mode.
  4. Preview the content before generating the report.
  5. Once generated, view, share, or save the PDF.

Immutable history

Each generated report is kept as an independent version. Generating a new report does not modify previous versions.

Report already exists

If an identical report already exists for the same baby, date range, and mode, Baby Companion lets you open it or generate a new version.

STEP 9

Baby Companion Assistant

Quickly find information and prepare certain actions.

  • The Assistant uses the active baby’s context to answer.
  • You can switch babies using the Assistant context selector.
  • Switching babies starts a new conversation to avoid mixing contexts.
  • Any action that writes information to the journal must be confirmed by the parent.

No medical diagnosis

The Assistant can summarize information already recorded and suggest actions, but it does not provide a medical diagnosis.

STEP 10

Notifications

Targeted reminders without multiplying alerts.

  • Planned meal: notification 15 minutes before the scheduled time.
  • If the meal is still not recorded, a single follow-up may be sent 45 minutes later.
  • Consultation: reminder 24 hours before, with a suggestion to prepare the report if needed.
  • Daily check-in: around 20:00 only if something is still incomplete.

Quiet hours

Non-urgent reminders respect quiet hours from 21:00 to 08:00.

Multiple babies

The baby’s first name is included in the notification to avoid ambiguity.

STEP 11

Account, privacy, and security

Manage your parent profile without changing your babies’ data.

My account

  • Edit your first name, optional last name, and email address.
  • Changes to the parent account never modify baby profiles.
  • Deleting the account is a sensitive action that requires explicit confirmation.

Privacy

  • Parent-account data and each baby’s data are kept distinct.
  • The Assistant uses only the context needed for the requested answer or action.
  • Sharing is initiated explicitly by the parent.
  • The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are available from Settings.

Sign out

Signing out does not mean deleting

Signing out only ends the session. Adam, Lina, or any other baby profiles and their histories remain associated with the account.

STEP 12

Good practices and quick help

A few guidelines for using Baby Companion with confidence.

  • Record at the right time — Use Planning for the future and Meals for what actually happened.
  • Keep observations factual — For a reaction or health note, describe what you observe without interpreting it.
  • Check the active baby — Before adding data, check the first name displayed in the header.
  • Prepare for the consultation — Before an appointment, review the measurements and generate a Diversification Report if useful.

Need help?

  • Start with the relevant screen: the main actions are shown directly.
  • Use the Assistant to find information already recorded or to understand where to perform an action.
  • For a medical question or a concerning situation, contact a healthcare professional.

Baby Companion in one sentence

A living journal that helps parents organize food diversification and daily tracking while keeping a clear history for consultations.
